# Texas Hold'em - Rules

## Goal of the Game

Texas Hold'em is a poker variant where each player tries to make the best 5-card hand using their private hole cards and the community cards, or to make all other players fold through betting.

This game is played with chip stacks. A player with no chips left after a completed hand is eliminated. Play continues until only one player still has chips.

## Cards and Hand Building

The game uses a standard 52-card deck without jokers.

Each active player receives 2 private hole cards. Up to 5 community cards are dealt face up in the middle of the table. From the 7 available cards, the best combination of exactly 5 cards counts.

## Hand Flow

1. The dealer button determines the table positions.
2. Small blind and big blind are posted automatically.
3. Each active player receives 2 private hole cards.
4. Preflop betting round.
5. Flop: one card is burned, then 3 community cards are revealed.
6. Flop betting round.
7. Turn: one card is burned, then 1 community card is revealed.
8. Turn betting round.
9. River: one card is burned, then 1 community card is revealed.
10. River betting round.
11. Showdown, if at least 2 non-folded players remain.

After each hand, the dealer button moves to the next player with chips. With exactly 2 active players, the dealer is also the small blind.

## Actions in a Betting Round

- Fold: give up your hand.
- Check: continue without betting if no bet is open.
- Call: match the open bet.
- Raise: increase the bet.
- All-in: commit all remaining chips.

A betting round ends when all non-folded and non-all-in players have acted and hold the same current bet.

## No-Limit Hold'em

In no-limit mode, a player may bet or raise up to their full stack.

- Preflop, the big blind is the open bet.
- Postflop, the minimum opening bet is the big blind.
- The minimum raise is based on the last full raise.
- All-in is always possible while the player still has chips.

Example with 10/20 blinds:

- A raise to 60 raises by 40.
- The next minimum raise is to 100.

## Fixed-Limit Hold'em

In fixed-limit mode, bets and raises use fixed sizes.

The two configured values are treated as small bet and big bet.

- Preflop and flop: bet size = small bet.
- Turn and river: bet size = big bet.
- The blinds are half the small bet and the full small bet.

Example with 20/40:

- Small blind = 10
- Big blind = 20
- Preflop and flop: raise step 20
- Turn and river: raise step 40

Each street allows at most 3 raises after the first bet. An all-in below the fixed bet size is only possible if the player's stack is not enough for a full call.

## Pot-Limit Hold'em

In pot-limit mode, raises are capped by the pot size.

- Postflop, the minimum opening bet is the big blind.
- The minimum raise is based on the last full raise.
- The maximum raise is calculated from pot + required call.
- All-in is allowed if it is no more than a call or stays within the pot-limit cap.

## All-in and Side Pots

When a player is all-in, they can only win pots they contributed chips to. If other players commit more chips afterward, side pots are created.

Each pot is resolved separately. Only players who have not folded and are eligible for that pot can win it.

Tied pots are split evenly. Odd leftover chips are awarded by seat order, starting left of the dealer.

If all remaining players except at most one can no longer commit more chips, the remaining community cards are revealed immediately and the hand goes straight to showdown.

## Showdown and Winning

At showdown, all non-folded players compare their best 5-card hand. The best hand wins the relevant pot.

If all but one player fold, the remaining player wins the pot without showdown.

After the hand, won chips are distributed. Players without chips are eliminated. The game ends as soon as only one player still has chips.
